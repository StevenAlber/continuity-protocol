The math behind the feeling

The International Committee of the Red Cross counts 130 active armed conflicts worldwide — the highest since 1945. Twice what it was 15 years ago. Nearly four times what it was 30 years ago.

Every one of these conflicts touches something downstream:

Banking

Jurisdictions freezing accounts, changing compliance rules, restricting transfers with days of notice.

Mobility

18,000+ flights cancelled in the Gulf crisis first week. Corridors close by Friday.

Supply chains

Hormuz shipping insurance tripled. Oil up 54% in 30 days. Energy grids under pressure.

Jurisdiction

Tax rules change overnight. Residency programs tighten. What was safe 18 months ago isn't.

The most dangerous position in 2026 is not being in the wrong country. It's having no plan for when the right one stops working.

What's inside the briefing

Each module is a video briefing (15–20 min) paired with a working operational document you can use immediately.

Module 01 · Video + PDF

The Real Risk Map

Not which countries are "dangerous" — every country is dangerous in some dimension. This maps which specific risks are accelerating in your likely jurisdictions: banking seizure, tax regime shift, mobility restriction, infrastructure failure, currency event. With timelines, not speculation.

Module 02 · Video + Decision Flowchart

The Decision Sequence

The exact order of operations for someone who has assets, family, and business exposure across unstable ground. What to secure first. What can wait. What looks urgent but isn't. What looks fine but will lock you in. This is the core of the briefing — the sequence that replaces panic with method.

Module 03 · Video + Jurisdiction Matrix

The Base Selection Grid

Not "best countries to move to." Instead: which combinations of two or three bases give you maximum operational continuity if any single one fails. Banking, schooling, healthcare, legal structure, exit routes.

Module 04 · Video + Assessment Framework

The Trust Filter

Which categories of service providers — lawyers, banks, trustees, relocation agents — are actually functional right now. And which are selling 2019 solutions into a 2026 world. How to verify before you commit your family's safety to someone's sales pitch.

Module 05 · PDF Action Document

The 90-Day Operational Calendar

Week-by-week: what to move, what to open, what to close, what to test, what to digitize, who to call. Built for someone who has a life and a business and cannot stop everything to "figure this out."
